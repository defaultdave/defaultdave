summary: A web app that visualizes scales on a diatonic harmonica. Pick a harmonica key, a song key and a scale, and it shows which holes to play, bends and overblows included, across five tunings and 12 scale types. I built the harmonica layout on tonal.js, audio playback on the Web Audio API, a chord explorer, a key identification quiz, and PNG and PDF export.
